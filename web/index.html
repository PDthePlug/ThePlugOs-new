GET YOUR DEVICES TOGETHER

Your first test order.
Four simple steps.

You need two Android devices, a working local Wi-Fi network, and power for the hub. Internet is needed to download the app; the local order loop uses shop Wi-Fi.

01 /

Choose your hub

Install the pilot on the cashier device. Initialize one training hub and choose different manager, cashier and kitchen PINs.

02 /

Connect the kitchen

Join the same Wi-Fi and install the app. Sign in as manager on the hub, then create a kitchen enrollment code. Compare the full certificate fingerprint before pairing.

03 /

Send a test order

Sign in as cashier, add food and notes, then send. On the kitchen device, acknowledge the order, start preparing, and mark it ready.

04 /

Complete the handover

Check the persistent Ready indicator at the till, then confirm collection. Next, repeat the cycle with internet disconnected and Wi-Fi still working.

i

Keep the kitchen workspace open and signed in. Background kitchen delivery is not implemented in this pilot. A device without a hub connection cannot confirm an order.
